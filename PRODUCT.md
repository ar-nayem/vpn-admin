# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People purchasing a simple personal VPN, often from a phone, tablet, or desktop. The administrator reviews manual WeChat Pay and Alipay payments and manages VPN access without interrupting connected customers.

## Product Purpose

Bright Pocket VPN lets a verified email start one free 1 GB trial, purchase a monthly plan, submit payment proof, receive an AmneziaWG configuration, and track remaining data. Success means customers can complete these tasks without technical help while the live VPN remains available.

## Positioning

One email can securely manage named device profiles, and a paid purchase upgrades the same free-trial VPN identity so the customer does not reconnect a new configuration.

## Operating Context

Customers scan WeChat Pay or Alipay QR codes, upload a payment screenshot, wait for administrator review, receive a branded email with a `.conf` attachment and secure link, then import it into the AmneziaWG app. Guests return using six-digit email verification; registered customers use a dashboard.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Basic: ¥5 per month, 60 GB per month, 5 Mbps upload and download.
- Premium: ¥10 per month, 120 GB per month, unlimited speed.
- Pro: ¥15 per month, 200 GB per month, unlimited speed.
- Multi-month purchases multiply price and total quota; quota spans the full term without monthly reset.
- One verified email receives one automatic 1 GB, 5 Mbps, no-expiry trial for one device.
- Registered customers may manage multiple unique code names; each code name represents one device and VPN profile.
- Paid orders require a payment screenshot and administrator approval.
- Email verification uses six-digit codes. Accounts support password login, code login, and password recovery.
- The private administrator interface shares Bright Pocket's cream canvas, paper surfaces, purple actions and structure, and yellow metrics and emphasis while remaining denser, task-first, private, and functionally compatible. VPN changes use targeted operations and never restart or reload the live interface.

## Brand Commitments

The customer product is named Bright Pocket VPN. Its approved direction uses a warm cream base, energetic yellow feature surfaces, vivid purple actions, friendly plain language, and a colorful professional character. The private admin uses the same cream, paper, purple, and yellow visual language in a denser, task-first expression without changing its functional behavior or privacy boundary.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the approved product specification and implementation plan. No customer testimonials, performance benchmarks, payment QR images, logo artwork, or promotional photography have been supplied; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make plan limits and totals impossible to misunderstand.
- Keep customer identity and VPN data private by default.
- Preserve the same device configuration when upgrading whenever possible.
- Treat payment approval, VPN provisioning, and email delivery as separate recoverable steps.
- Keep every customer flow comfortable on phone, tablet, and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, touch targets of at least 44 pixels, responsive layouts without horizontal overflow, and status text that does not rely on color alone.

Scope: private Bright Pocket administrator workspace. Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: the administrator must manage live VPN users and device limits, review storefront customers and orders, maintain payment QR assets, and inspect eligible usage history without interrupting connected customers.

Primary action: perform a targeted VPN or storefront operation against the correct record. Secondary actions: move between workspaces, inspect customer/profile analytics, recover from loading failures, and maintain administrative access.

Truth and constraints: preserve live VPN availability; targeted mutations never imply or require a broad VPN restart or interface reload. Customer identity and analytics remain private, and usage workspaces list only eligible profiles. Exact transfer, speed, quota, expiry, payment, and connection facts must not be embellished.

Chosen direction: Bright Pocket operational ledger. The private admin shares the cream canvas, paper surfaces, purple actions/structure, yellow metrics/emphasis, pocket mark, and compact curves of the customer product, but expresses them with denser records and task-first hierarchy.

Memorable moment: Usage history turns a selected customer/profile into four yellow fact tiles and a readable two-series timeline whose scale, zero baseline, time range, timezone, legend, and prose summary remain explicit.

Implementation grammar: 1800px maximum workspace; system UI for body, tables, inputs, timestamps, and measurements; Anybody only for brand, headings, navigation, and actions; 10px controls, 12px buttons/metrics, 14px charts, and 16px panels/dialogs; 44px minimum targets; visible purple focus; flat tonal depth; no gradients, glass, or decorative blur. Keep comparison tables on wide screens, transform records into labeled cards at 1100px and below, and stack selectors/controls below 760px.

Chart grammar: native SVG with points spaced by valid timestamps; solid Action Purple upload and dashed Anchor Purple download; maximum and zero baseline above; start/midpoint/end labels below; accessible `<title>`/`<desc>`, visible legend, status/error/retry copy, and full text summary. Respect reduced motion.

Unresolved: none in the shipped visual system; future admin surfaces should extend this grammar instead of reviving the legacy dark theme.

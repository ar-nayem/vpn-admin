Scope: public storefront landing page and shared customer surfaces. Mode: Persuade on landing; Operate on authentication, checkout, tracking, dashboard, and customer usage analytics.

Audience and job: phone-, tablet-, and desktop-based customers who need to understand exact VPN limits, start one free trial, pay manually, and recover or track access without technical help.

Primary action: Start 1 GB free. Secondary actions: compare exact plans, sign in, or verify email to track a guest package.

Truth and constraints: packages and trial facts come from PRODUCT.md; no testimonials, benchmark claims, server-location claims, or payment imagery may be invented. Customer and private admin now intentionally share the Bright Pocket operational-ledger language, while admin stays denser and task-first. Customer usage remains private to the signed-in customer's eligible profiles.

Chosen direction: Bright Pocket payment ledger, with a yellow offer field, deep purple actions, cream paper, and exact yellow–purple–deep-purple plan progression. Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/storefront-ledger.png`; use its composition only because its phone art and several claims are illustrative and not product truth.

Memorable moment: the first viewport pairs “One VPN file. Upgrade without reconnecting.” with a clearly labeled trial preview that visibly keeps the same connection.

Implementation grammar: 12–16px corners, borderless color fields, soft offset elevation only where it helps the customer hierarchy, compressed heavy Anybody for brand/headings/navigation/actions, plain system body and measurement type, 2px outlined secondary controls, and 44px minimum targets. Customer analytics uses yellow summary metrics plus a native SVG chart with timestamp spacing, max/zero/time context, solid upload, dashed download, accessible naming, and a visible text summary. Motion is limited to a status pulse and removed for reduced-motion users.
